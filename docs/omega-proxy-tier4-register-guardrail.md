## Rule — register-by-fit, per objection
Author each tier-4 position to its objection's native register. The analytic/experiential split is a **heuristic for where clinical stops fitting**, not a two-register roster — buddhist ≠ transhumanist ≠ care-ethics in fit.

- **Cold-analytic objections** (equivocation, category-error, counterfactual-identity, structurally similar formal attacks): **Personal-Clinical** — cold grant-then-mislocate. This is *fit*, not monoculture; importing affect reads as changing the subject and brushes the appraisal-silent fence. Do **not** flag a uniformly-clinical *analytic* batch.
- **Experiential / tradition-framed objections** (meaning-through-suffering, phenomenological, buddhist, care-ethics, virtue-ethics, indigenous, flow-states, hedonic-contrast, neuroscience-positive-states, transhumanist, pragmatist): **warmth-by-fit** — Ethical-spine cadence / Personal warmth, chosen per objection, never a single warm default. Pure clinical distance here can read as conceding the objector's point.

## Appraisal-silent stance-fence (governs the warm registers)
Appraisal-silent is **stance-scoped, not affect-scoped** (K266): it bars *positive value-appraisal of existence* and *directive force* — not tone. Warmth is permitted, provided every register stays **rebut-only**:

- Rebut the objection's **inference** (e.g., "lived meaning ⇒ existence is justified").
- Do **not** grant the felt-content as positive value (that the suffering itself is meaningful/good).
- Do **not** counsel the objector ("you should reframe / you should bear it").
- Warmth lives in cadence and engagement, never in a value-claim. A warm rebut that affirms meaning, or advises the reader, has breached the fence → de-warm or re-scope.
