## TITLE

```
The Argument Library — 82 objections to antinatalism, graded and answered
```

72 characters. Both search terms in the first six words, the tool named, the method stated.

**What changed and why.** The live title is *"every objection to antinatalism, in one place."*
**"Every" is a completeness claim the film does not make and the coda explicitly refuses.** The
library catalogues eighty-two; it does not assert that eighty-two is all of them, and the whole
register of the film is a catalogue that names its own limits. A title that overclaims is the one
thing a viewer can check in four minutes, and the Apparatus spends three thousand words being careful
about exactly this. Say the number.

It also stops duplicating the thumbnail. The plate now reads **82 OBJECTIONS / TO ANTINATALISM**, so
the title's job is the tool's name and what it does with them, not a second copy of the subject.
