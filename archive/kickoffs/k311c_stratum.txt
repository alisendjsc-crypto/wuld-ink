CCCVIII, THIRD AXIS (still no constants committed). The contrast axis they added was SIGNED and would
have suppressed every darkening treatment -- a vignette halving reads 0.5 where the LEDs read 4.4, so
any floor above 1.0 vetoes all darkening and any floor below compresses it into noise. The vignette is
already in this film's chain, so it was queued rather than hypothetical. Fixed on this seat's proposal
by measuring in STOPS, |log2(new/old)|, which is direction-blind:
    chin LEDs      0.145 -> 0.78     2.43 stops
    vignette       0.5x              1.00 stops
    bloom on pixels already at the top          0.08 stops
A floor near half a stop passes the first two and rejects the third. Their earlier absolute-brightness
version was worse than one-directional -- it compared display-referred PNG values (AgX lifts darks
hard; the chin reads 0.145 in a frame of mean 0.05-0.09) against a threshold picked as if the values
were scene-referred, and vetoed the LEDs for sitting on nearly-black ground.

THREE CORRECTIONS TO THAT DOCUMENT, in the section it tells the reader to read first:
1. ITS AMPLITUDE ADVICE IS INVERTED. It says a 10%-of-peak-white swing is "a very small number in
   absolute terms" on a dark palette. Relative luminance is normalised with 1.0 = peak white -- the
   correction they handed this seat in K310c, applied to their own paragraph -- and gamma compresses
   the dark end: from a #0E0E10 ground (L ~ 0.004) a legal 0.10 swing reaches sRGB ~#5B5B5B, near-black
   to mid-grey. A DARK PAGE HAS MORE PERCEPTUAL ROOM UNDER THE CRITERION THAN A LIGHT ONE, not less.
2. TWO OF THE FOUR FLASH GATES ARE AUTOMATIC HERE, NOT ONE. They correctly note a page-wide effect
   satisfies the 25% AREA condition by construction. The criterion also requires the darker state below
   relative luminance 0.80, which every state on this palette is. Only amplitude and rate remain.
3. ITS §10 GATES THE MASCOT'S FADE ON prefers-reduced-motion. Wrong switch: that preference targets
   vestibular triggers (parallax, zoom, translation), and a slow cross-fade is what the guidance
   recommends SUBSTITUTING for motion. Gating opacity on it makes first paint a static bezel, less
   alive than the film Josiah asked to adopt. The line falls between vestibular and opacity.
Also flagged, not a safety item: a live filter firing loading/loaded per keystroke drives the LEDs at
4-6 Hz and every transition is "required by the state," which is exactly the justification their rule
accepts. Debounce the LED to the state's MEANING, not to the event.
