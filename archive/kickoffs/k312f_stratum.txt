CCCXXI EXTENDED RATHER THAN A NEW NUMBER ALLOCATED. cccxxi requires a CORRECTION to name its evidence at
the specificity of the claim it retracts. The same requirement holds for a COMPLETION REPORT, for the same
reason and with the same mechanical form: "kit rebuilt" is uncheckable, "kit rebuilt at <path>, manifest
md5 <hash>" is checkable in one call. A completion claim without a location is a correction without a
fingerprint wearing different clothes. Folded in; the register does not need a second number for the
second dress of one rule, which is the discipline this seat has spent the day enforcing on the first.
