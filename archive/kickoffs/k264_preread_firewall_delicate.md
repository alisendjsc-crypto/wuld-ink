### pos-violence-as-reductio-01
*objection* `violence-as-reductio` · *angle* genetic-fallacy / interchangeable-template · *volta* `He charges` · *image* authorship of a deed (charged to its doer, not a shared premise) · *655 chars*

> An argument and the people who carry it are separate things to refute. To bring the case down you meet its premises: that a beginning fixes suffering on someone who could not be asked. To bring an adherent down you meet the adherent. Point to the loudest follower and you have named a follower, not an entailment, since the act took commitments the premises never issued. Watch the dreaded outcome swap from one catastrophe to the next, and you hold a template that found its disaster after the fact. He charges the deed to the hand that did it and lifts it off a sentence a stranger also holds. A frame that fits any catastrophe was never about this one.

*forms:* `your followers prove your framework` (w3), `look at your extreme adherents` (w3), `your movement has no brakes` (w2)

*firewall:* Separates the argument from any actor; names and describes no violent act; shows the reductio is a template that swaps its catastrophe and never meets the premises. Endorses nothing; third-party-harm-free; no crisis vocabulary.

### pos-violence-as-reductio-02
*objection* `violence-as-reductio` · *angle* mechanism is the opposite of violence · *volta* `He sifts` · *image* a blow that must reach a living body vs. an abstention that reaches no one · *661 chars*

> Grant the darkest reading of the conclusion and the charge still points the wrong way. Violence is an act that lands on a body already here, a will already running, someone who can be wronged. The refusal at issue lands on no one, because before a beginning there is no one in its path to be reached. What the case asks is that a life not be started, and a life not started has no bearer to suffer the not starting. One act moves toward a person, the other leaves the place a person would have stood empty. He sifts the act that needs a victim from the act that can never find one. To read a refusal to create as a blow, you have to invent the body it falls on.

*forms:* `your view leads to violence` (w3), `one logical step from atrocity` (w3), `the logical endpoint is catastrophe` (w2)

*firewall:* Violence named only in the abstract to CONTRAST; argues the position (a life not started) is the structural opposite of a blow, reaching no one. December-rule: speaks of not-beginning, never of ending a life or death-as-relief. Endorses no violence.

### pos-slippery-slope-eugenics-01
*objection* `slippery-slope-eugenics` · *angle* selection / no group · *volta* `He sorts` · *image* a machine sorting a population into fit/unfit · *654 chars*

> Set the two beside each other and they run in opposite directions. The one you name sorts people first: it marks some as fit to reproduce and others as unfit, then presses the first to breed while the rest are stopped. Every move it makes rests on the line drawn between kinds of people. The claim in front of you draws no such line. It reaches the most favored life there is on the same terms as any other, because its objection is to imposing a life at all, not to which sort of life gets imposed. He sorts the program that ranks its subjects from a claim that holds them all alike. Picking out a group is the whole of the one and no part of the other.

*forms:* `this is a slippery slope to eugenics` (w3), `sounds like eugenics` (w3), `this is eugenics` (w2)

*firewall:* Eugenics' sorting described to CONDEMN and contrast; the claim draws no line, selects no group, reaches the most-favored life on the same terms. Endorses no eugenics; coerces no one.

### pos-slippery-slope-eugenics-02
*objection* `slippery-slope-eugenics` · *angle* coercion / who is addressed · *volta* `He addresses` · *image* the direction of a demand (a program on bodies vs. a claim to the actor) · *661 chars*

> A second gap hides under the resemblance, in who the thing is done to. A program of that kind is worked on other bodies: it sterilizes, licenses, forbids, sets the state or the crowd over a person's reproduction and enforces the ruling. The claim here issues no such order. It is addressed to the one weighing the act, about that act, and asks for no registry, no force, no hand laid on anyone who walks away. It moves by reasons put to a chooser, never by power worked on the chosen. He addresses the case to the hand that would create and lays it on no body a program would compel. A reason put to a decision is the far pole from force worked on a population.

*forms:* `sounds like population control` (w3), `population control` (w3), `this is nazi eugenics` (w2)

*firewall:* Coercion (sterilize/forbid) named only to CONTRAST; the claim asks for no force, no registry, no hand laid on anyone. Endorses no eugenics or coercion.

### pos-ableist-objection-01
*objection* `ableist-objection` · *angle* universality collapses the selectivity charge · *volta* `He levels` · *image* one measure leveled identically across unlike lives · *680 chars*

> The claim never reads a diagnosis. It falls with its whole weight on the child promised ninety-five healthy years exactly as on any other, because what it weighs is the act of setting a life going for someone who could not be asked, never the contents of the life set going. A verdict that lands the same on the most fortunate life there is cannot be a verdict about disability, since to reach the disabled it would have to rank lives by condition, and it ranks none. No life here is named lesser, because no life here is graded at all. He levels one measure across every life and shows it was never a measure of the life. The worth of a life lived is a question it does not open.

*forms:* `this is ableist` (w3), `your view is ableist` (w3), `that sounds ableist` (w2)

*firewall:* AFFIRMS no life is graded or named lesser; the identical verdict falls on the guaranteed-healthy life, so it cannot single out disability. Never devalues disabled lives.

### pos-ableist-objection-02
*objection* `ableist-objection` · *angle* category/tense swap · *volta* `He predates` · *image* time / precedence (before-anyone vs. a-life-underway) · *686 chars*

> The objection reads the claim in the wrong tense. Not worth living is a sentence passed on a life underway, a person whose days are being counted and found short. The claim never enters that room. It speaks to the act of beginning a life, before anyone exists to be weighed, before any trait, sound or otherwise, has arrived to count. Whoever is already living, of any health, stands outside its reach. The devaluation it dreads is a verdict on the living, the objection's own, read into a claim that only ever spoke of a beginning. He predates the whole case to before the person, where a judgment on a life lived has nothing to stand on. What is never weighed cannot be found wanting.

*forms:* `this devalues disabled people` (w3), `your view devalues the disabled` (w3), `you are saying disabled people should not be born` (w2)

*firewall:* 'Not worth living' is quoted as the OBJECTION's verdict and disowned as its own supply; affirms the already-living of any health are outside the claim's reach. Never devalues; December-rule clean.
