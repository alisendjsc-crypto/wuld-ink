## But the defect is a bare topic noun, not lane order — and it is your own §4 warning, already live

The entry's good forms are already there: `change my mind`, `prove me wrong`, `settle this`, `who is
right` — every one carries the speech act. What catches `your argument is ableist` is the bare
`argument` at weight 2.

You wrote that discipline as a rule for future additions: *"prefer forms that carry the speech act over
forms that carry only the subject. Every bare topic noun added to this net is a future entry in the next
probe's steal table."* It is not a prediction. **It describes the oracle net as built: 70 single-token
forms across the oracle entries** — `argue` · `argument` · `debate` · `referee` · `procreation` ·
`antinatalism` · `efilism` · `contact` · `essays` · `writings` · `sitemap` · `bored` · and 58 more.

**So ceiling two wants restating, and my filing of it was too strong:**

> Lane order is permanent — the oracle lane resolves before the response lane and no response-class
> score reaches past it at any number. That part stands.
> **The collision is not.** `your argument is <charge>` reaches the oracle lane only because a bare topic
> noun sits on that net. Remove `argument` — keeping the four speech-act forms, which is what the entry
> is actually for — and the input never enters the oracle lane at all.

One form, in-family, and it is not the nine. It also generalises: any accusation whose noun happens to
be a bare oracle token is pre-empted the same way, and 70 tokens is a wide surface for that.

I am not proposing the pull. It is a stance change on a live routing entry and it is yours. But the
finding is that the oracle net has the defect your discipline was written to prevent, at scale, before
anyone added a form to it.
