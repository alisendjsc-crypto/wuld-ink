## 2 · The arithmetic the three examples concealed

The bait's fragment is `your <noun>`, and the bar it sets is whatever that fragment measures:

```
your view          9        your opinion        12
your take          9        your position       13
your stance       11        your thoughts on    16
do you think      12        how do you feel about  21
```

Now the bare charges the nine actually contain:

```
depression talking  18  ✓ beats your take 9
first world problem 19  ✓
misanthropic        12    beats your stance 11 — loses to your thoughts on 16
privileged          10  ✗ loses to your opinion 12
nihilistic          10  ✗
eugenics             8  ✗ loses to your position 13
nihilism             8  ✗
ableist              7  ✗
selfish              7  ✗
```

**Your three examples are the long end of the distribution.** The charges in the nine are mostly bare adjectives, and an adjective has no modifier-slot-free multiword form — the moment you extend it you cross the copula and become adverb-fragile again. `is selfish` is 10 and still loses; `position is selfish` spans the slot. There is no form length that satisfies both conditions for those five.

## 3 · And the bar is per-input, which makes length unusable as an instrument

This is the decisive part. `misanthropic` at 12 **wins** against `your stance` and **loses** against `your thoughts on`. The competing fragment is a property of the sentence the user typed, not of the entry. So there is no form length that is safe across the input space — only lengths that are safe against the inputs you happened to probe.

Choosing forms by length is therefore fitting to a sample, one more time.

**Weight is input-independent.** Patterns carry their own weight; the bait's flat 63 is `BASE.contains 60 + 3`. A charge form at **weight 4 scores 64 and wins outright** — no length contest, no per-input bar, and unlike `exact` it still generalizes across phrasings because it stays `contains`.

That is the same move your `exact` insight made — escape the 63 tie rather than win inside it — without `exact`'s brittleness.

**The gate, because raising weight is exactly how the bait got here.** A bare `eugenics` at 64 captures every sentence containing the word, including *what is eugenics* and *eugenics was discredited*. That is the bait's own pathology relocated. So weight is licensed **only** on forms that are inherently accusatory — which is your charge-phrase criterion, doing the work it is actually good for. It was never a length rule; it is the *safety* condition on raising weight.

**So the conjunction:**

1. **Inherently accusatory** — carries the speech act. Your charge-phrase criterion. Licenses (3).
2. **No internal modifier slot** — sits downstream of the copula. Your position finding.
3. **Escapes the 63 contest** — weight 4 where the charge is short, which is most of them. Length only where the phrase is naturally long *and* you accept it may lose against a longer fragment in a sentence you have not seen.

`depression talking` satisfies all three today by accident of length. `selfish` needs weight.

## 4 · What to measure before writing anything

The weight move has a blast radius and it should be measured the way everything else in this thread eventually was.

- **Neutral-query control.** For each charge form you intend to weight up, route the neutral and definitional phrasings — *what is eugenics*, *is that ableist*, *nihilism is incoherent*, *he was being selfish about it*. If a weighted form captures those, it has become the bait.
- **Cross-entry contest.** A charge form at 64 now outranks every other `contains` entry at 63, not just the bait. Check what else it takes — particularly inside the same family, and particularly the oracle lane where `ORACLE_MIN` may now be cleared by something that should not clear it.
- **Adverb sweep on the proposed forms**, not on the six adverbs. Pick them from a source other than the finding: a frequency list, or an adversary. Otherwise ccxcvi fires a fifth time.
