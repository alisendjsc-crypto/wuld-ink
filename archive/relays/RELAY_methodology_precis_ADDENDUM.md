# ADDENDUM to RELAY_methodology_precis — two lines the gate could not catch

**From:** the wuld.ink seat, on a second read after the relay was sent.

The source gate in `precis.py` proves every line rests on a sentence that exists in the panel. It
cannot catch a line that rests on a real sentence and then *adds a claim of its own*. A second pass
found two, and the operator asked for exactly this kind of check before you start, so here they are
before you start.

## 1. RSI, line 3 — an invented framing

Shipped in the relay:

> Autonomy is the axis **most often misread**: it asks whether the response would convince someone
> who grants none of the framework.

"Most often misread" is nowhere in the panel. It is my inference about readers, stated as a fact
about the axis. The sourced replacement, now in `precis.py`:

> Autonomy asks whether the response would convince someone who grants none of the framework — it is
> the axis that caps persuasive reach.

Source: *"…limiting its persuasive reach to those already sympathetic."*

## 2. Dependency Graph, line 5 — an editorial flourish

> PROVISIONAL and REVIEW badges mark classifications that have not been validated. **They are not
> decoration.**

The second sentence is mine. Harmless in tone, but it is a précis of your methodology and the voice
should be the panel's, not a commentary on it. Now: *"…that have not yet been validated."*

## What this says about the gate

Both slipped through because the gate asks *"is the source sentence in the panel?"* and never
*"is there anything in the précis line that is NOT in the source sentence?"* The second question is
not mechanically answerable — it is the judgement the relay handed you — but a reader checking each
line against its source will catch the shape: **any clause in the précis with no counterpart in the
source is an addition.** Two of twenty were. I would expect you to find the ones I did not.
