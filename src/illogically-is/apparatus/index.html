This document is the film's only credit sequence: the film carries none, by rule — its own apparatus (leader numerals, sprocket counts, the section headings, the footnote marks) decays across the running time the way the footnotes of the source chapter do. What the picture refuses to make legible, this page does.

Illogically Is (also Alogical Isness) — essay film, 36:12 (2,172.17 s; 52,080 frames at 23.976 fps), 1920 × 1080, Rec.709, stereo. The voice runs 35:53.85 and enters at 0:16.5; the picture outlives it by eighteen seconds. The film contains sustained stroboscopic flashing and high-contrast flicker and opens on a six-second warning card; a reduced-flash edition is planned as a separate, labelled file. Closed captions (subs_synced.srt, 283 cues on the film's own clock) are supplied with every upload.

1. Sources

1.1 The text and the voice

The film is a reading of the treatise Alogical Isness — ten sections, three movements with a prologue and a coda — in a single synthetic voice (ElevenLabs text-to-speech, a fixed 44.1 kHz mono spine, 2,153.854 s, never cut). The voice is the ceiling of the film and stays; the whole pipeline is derived from the waveform, so a second edition with a human reading is a day of renders, not a rebuild — a decision for after the release. The register of the picture comes from a second text: Lacero, the experimental section of Malgré Tout: a Guide to Nothingness (W.U.L.D.), read not as a chapter about tearing but as the record of a clinical register failing to hold. The origin document of both is a dream written down on 18 June 2010 — two points of dim red oblivion — whose sentences appear in the film one frame at a time, in the silences between sections, in the order they were written.

1.2 The films quoted

Four films are quoted as themselves — recognisable on screen, torn but named: Persona, The Flicker, La Jetée, In Absentia. They are the whitelist: faces the film could not synthesise under its own rule against new generations; the historical stroboscope; the stills-film that is the ancestor of the film's generated stills; the Quays' writing machine. Seven further films are the reserve, allowed only below the lacero threshold — a destruction of at least 0.6 (the sum of the operations applied), never a readable shot — and they are credited here precisely because the picture never lets them be seen: the stones are copies of stones. Every window below is the source range actually cut (the film's timecode), the section that called for it, its strand in the braid (citation = the film as film; torch = the plates' and stills' strand, where a reserve film stood in for a plate; clinical = the film's own generated masters), and the reason recorded in the score at the time. No cited source appears at the quality it had: on every return it is a copy of its previous appearance.

2. Method

2.1 Three pictures and a bed, at zero

The film is four files dropped at 00:00:00:00 on the clock of the voice recording and never moved: L1, the picture behind the words (lacero.mp4, 51,641 frames, opaque); L2, the text picture (subs_essence_s16.mp4, 52,080 frames), composited in Screen so that it can only add light; L3, the analog layer — two pictures, analog_screen.mp4 (Screen: grain's positive half, dust and scratches on the negative, splices, cue dots, perforations, the burns' light, the embers) and analog_multiply.mp4 (Multiply, outermost: the gate and vignette, dark scratches and dirt, the hair, the frame line, the projector's flicker, the scorch, the douser); and the bed, one 48 kHz stereo file carrying the mix. The master is one ffmpeg graph — L1 padded with black to L2's length ⊕ L2 ⊕ L3-screen ⊗ L3-multiply, in linear RGB before every blend — with the bed muxed as AAC 256 k (an archival ProRes 422 HQ / PCM master is composed from the same graph). The Multiply layer is outermost because the douser at No self must black the words as well as the picture.

2.2 The grammar

Every operation in every layer cites one of thirteen rules read out of the Lacero chapter — the chapter as picture, not the word — and five refusals. Compressed:

  1. The picture is the clinical voice. Composed, slow, low-pass, clean; lacero is that composure slipping, a gradient with local reversals, never a switch.
  2. The seam is the enemy. No cuts and no dissolves — the therefore that lets a next sentence arrive; every transition is the noise the outgoing image makes while collapsing, until the next image is simply there.
  3. The voice is an auto-opposer. Whatever the image reaches for, the picture opposes — a face its negative, a still moves, motion freezes, light inverts. The oscillation is the drag.
  4. Citation is memory of citation. Each return of a source is a copy of its previous appearance; the base stone is missing.
  5. One image is exempt — the corridor, the cat on the table: unchanged on return, never processed.
  6. The inventory surfaces against decision. The plates, the two points, the figures appear for one to three frames, unbidden, when a whitelisted film is on screen.
  7. The word breaks in the mouth. The cue's own words, rasterised huge, are cut out of the image — a glyph-shaped wound, never legible — 0.7 s before the voice (after it in Cause and Effect), misspelling on every repetition.
  8. The braid. Three strands — clinical, citation, torch — composited by difference so that where they align they go to black.
  9. The ride (Mazeppa). The strobe is two poles not chosen, at a gallop, its rate following the syllables; in the Gridlock the horse slows.
  10. Under- and over-dense. Frames alternate starved (black, a single point, one hair) and glutted; after the rupture the margins collapse inward and the image rides too high.
  11. Lowercase after the rupture. Nothing sits full-frame and clean; the apparatus decays like the footnotes — See —, —, blank.
  12. The primate inside the filter. The low-pass image is the filter; its high frequencies leak through in proportion to the arc; by Remains the scream is audible inside it.
  13. Malgré Tout. It does not end on black: the ride ends, the horse stands, the fire is still lit — one ember, the two points dimmed to one, survives HAEC VERBA MENTIUNTUR.

The refusals: no legible text in the picture layer, ever; no clean cut and no dissolve; no symmetry; no image completes its motion; no cited source returns at the quality it had; nothing is announced. The arc: overture (Prologue, Logic) → the tightening slips (Cause, Void) → rupture (Consciousness → Language: the word is not the thing) → the braid collapses and the inventory surfaces (Self) → the ride (Remains, Gridlock: the gallop, then the horse slows) → the fire still lit (Coda). Intensity 0.05 → 0.95 → 0.1, monotone only on average.

2.3 The score

A YAML score (lacero_score.yaml, source_pools.yaml) fixes, per section, the intensity range, the source pools of the three strands and the section's own rule; the engine (gen_lacero.py, seed 2010) derives every frame from it deterministically, so the film can be re-rendered from the scripts to the pixel. The section rules as written:

  • §1 Why This Is Different — stills-only (La Jetee rule). Slow tears open to black. Filter holds completely.
  • §2 On Logic and Its Limits — binary flicker (tertium non datur) - white/black, no grey; hard-edged block scrambles; grids.
  • §3 On Cause and Effect — reversed footage; effect-before-cause wounds (offset +0.7 s, AFTER the word); strobe on the beat BEFORE each stressed word; tracking loss on "Hume".
  • §4 On the Void That Generates — black dominates; image born from noise and collapsing back; masks-peel; under-dense pages.
  • §5 On Consciousness and What It Cannot Know — Persona faces as negative self-overlays that never align; the image cannot see itself; double exposure with offset.
  • §6 On Language and Its Betrayal — the rupture. pixel-sort by luminance = sorted words; block shuffle; channel split; text sources torn illegible; datamosh deferred to S14.
  • §7 On the Self That Isn't — split faces merging; masks; the film-burn at "No self." (1293.4); then a new reel; the inventory surfaces hardest here.
  • §8 On What Remains — strip the image - edges -> lines -> points; the primate inside the filter; under-dense pages dominate.
  • §9 On Knowing Nothing & The Gridlock — grids; locked frames stuttering; the Morse strobe (SCIO ME NIHIL SCIRE, v2 - rides the gallop, legible as the horse slows); the corridor at length; the ride at full speed then the horse slows.
  • §10 On the Paradox of This Document — leader, countdown that does not count, sprocket holes; La Jetee stills; one true motion at "And yet - it is." (2149.7); the two points dimming to one ember.

2.4 Time

Nothing on screen is placed by a subtitle file. Every cue was re-timed from the audio itself: word timestamps from faster-whisper (small.en, without voice-activity gating, which drops phrases on this voice) matched 99.4 % of the treatise's 2,862 tokens; cue edges were then snapped to the speech runs of the 16 kHz RMS envelope (−45 dBFS), with a two-frame lead and a 0.35 s hold. Every cue begins within 75 ms of the voice; 99.0 % of speech time lies under its cue. The section markers are the first voiced line minus seven seconds. The same word table lights each word on screen as it is spoken.

2.5 The text picture

The words are the only thing on screen that holds. Each sentence condenses out of a violet-grey static, is read by a sweep that brightens each word as the voice reaches it, then rises and recedes as a trace while the next sentence arrives — the sentence's memory, designed to fade toward ember. Headings and Latin tags stand top-left in the register of a document; the unspoken Latin surfaces at the foot of the frame, where subtitles would be; a small cold telemetry counts the section, the time and the characters spoken. Where a bright clip floods the heading's box the tag flares ember for a beat and gives way — the image contesting the heading — and returns when the picture darkens. The last character of the film is the period of HAEC VERBA MENTIUNTUR, which drops on its own schedule and burns last.

2.6 The yield

The text layer never touches the image; the image makes room. Under the sentence block — read, frame by frame, from the finished text picture itself — the picture behind the words yields: its luminance is held under 30 % with a soft knee at 60 and a shoulder that lands every highlight under 41 %, feathered over 80 px, ramping in a quarter second ahead of the words and lingering after. Under the section heading the yield takes the shape of the heading's own letters, dilated and blurred into a shadow with no edge. Flat fields — the white slams, the black pages — are left alone: there is nothing to yield to, and the words are meant to go white with the film.

2.7 The governor

No second of the output may carry more than ten full-field luminance transitions (a change of the frame mean by more than 40 of 255). The engine refuses the eleventh flash in any second and holds the previous image; every composite is audited afterwards with the same measure, layer against layer, and the analog layer is shown to add none. The film opens on the warning card because the ceiling is a ceiling, not a cure.

2.8 The analog layer

The body of the film reacting: ten reels, one per section, with cue dots eight seconds and one second before each changeover; the changeovers go sloppy from Language on — flash frame, frame line, heavy heads, the cue dots doubled, late, missing — as the apparatus decays. At No self (21:33) the changeover fails: the emulsion blisters, browns and burns through over Persona's own burn; the film tears (one black frame); the lamp shows through the empty gate; the douser drops — black, the words included — and the new reel arrives late with the heaviest head in the film. Nothing touches the corridor or the inserts; every insert is spliced. The gate is fixed and the film weaves in it; the projector's flicker in the Gridlock is the Morse of the section's own tag, in phase with the gallop; the Coda's grain spells HAEC VERBA MENTIUNTUR at 0.22 s a unit. Grain exists only where the image is — the layer reads the finished picture's light and puts nothing on black or white. After the voice ends the layer carries the two points: the left fades, the right ignites at 36:03 and the emulsion burns outward from it — except around the period, which burns last — the lamp dies, and the right ember is the last frame.

2.9 The mix
